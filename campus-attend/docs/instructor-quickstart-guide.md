# CampusAttend: Instructor Quick-Start Guide
*A 2-Minute Quick Reference for College Faculty Members*

---

## 1. Connecting to CampusAttend
1. Connect your laptop, tablet, or smartphone to the authorized **School Faculty Wi-Fi** network.
2. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) and go to the school server address provided by IT:
   ```text
   http://attendance.school.lan:5173  (or http://192.168.1.100:5173)
   ```
3. Enter your assigned username and password on the Login screen.

---

## 2. Taking Classroom Attendance (Step-by-Step)

```
[1] Select Offering ➔ [2] Mark Roster ➔ [3] Review Counters ➔ [4] Submit & Lock
```

1. Click on the **"Take Attendance"** tab in the top navigation bar.
2. **Select Class & Date:**
   - Choose your course offering (e.g., `CS101 - BSCS 1-A`).
   - Select today's date. The student roster loads instantly.
3. **Marking Attendance:**
   - For classes where most students are present: Click **"Mark All Present"** to set all students to `Present` in 1 click.
   - For students who are absent, late, or excused: Click the corresponding pill button (`Present`, `Late`, `Absent`, `Excused`) next to their name.
   - Optional: Add notes or remarks in the text box (e.g., `Arrived 20 mins late with bus delay pass`).
4. **Final Submission:**
   - Click the green **"Submit Final Attendance"** button at the top right.
   - A summary dialog will appear showing the total headcount breakdown.
   - Click **"Confirm & Lock Roster"**.
   - The session will transition to a locked state to protect institutional integrity.

---

## 3. What to Do if You Need to Correct a Past Roster?
Because submitted attendance meetings are locked against accidental alteration:
1. Open the meeting under **"Take Attendance"**.
2. Find the student who requires an update.
3. Click the **Pencil (Correction)** icon next to their name.
4. Select the new status (e.g., `Excused`) and state the justification (e.g., `Student submitted official medical clinic slip`).
5. Click **"Submit Request"**.
6. Your Department Head will review and approve the request from their dashboard.

---

## 4. Downloading Attendance Sheets for Your Class Records
1. Navigate to the **"Reports"** tab.
2. Select your course offering.
3. Click **"Official PDF"** to download a formatted, printable institutional grade-sheet summary with official signatory blocks for your faculty portfolio.
4. Click **"Export CSV"** to open your class roll in Microsoft Excel.
